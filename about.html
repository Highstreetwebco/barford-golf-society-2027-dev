BARFORD, WARWICKSHIRE

Golf, with good company.

A friendly society for golfers of all abilities.

A society built on friendship

Barford Golf Society began with a group of local friends and a simple idea: enjoy great golf in a relaxed, friendly and social environment.

We welcome men and women of all ages and abilities. There’s friendly competition for those who want it, and good company for everyone.

Our golf days

We visit courses across Warwickshire and the Cotswolds, with a tradition of catching up over a drink at The Granville Arms afterwards.

You’ll find dates, venues, prices and everything you need for each golf day on the events page.
